# Business Problem

Olist needs to understand its sales performance, customer behavior,
product performance and customer experience in order to identify
opportunities for revenue growth and customer retention.

## Business Questions

1. How do revenue and order volume change over time?
Monthly revenue generally increased alongside order volume, peaking in November 2017. This indicates that revenue growth was primarily volume-driven, while fluctuations in average order value should be examined separately.

Monthly orders and revenue had a very strong positive correlation of 0.996. This suggests that revenue growth was primarily driven by increasing order volume, while average order value remained relatively stable.

2. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
Health and beauty is the highest-revenue product category, generating
approximately 1.26 million BRL, followed by watches and gifts at 1.21 million
BRL and bed, bath and table at 1.04 million BRL.

Watches and gifts achieves high revenue despite having fewer orders because
its average product price is relatively high. In contrast, bed, bath and table
has the highest number of orders and items sold among the top categories, but
its lower average product price places it third in revenue.

3. Which customer regions contribute the most sales?
São Paulo (SP) is the largest market, contributing approximately 37.41% of
total revenue, followed by Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and Minas Gerais (MG).

SP also has the shortest average delivery time among the top revenue-generating
states, at approximately 8.8 days, while Bahia (BA) has the longest at
approximately 19.3 days.

There is a moderate negative correlation between average delivery time and
total revenue (-0.600). The correlation remains similar after excluding SP
(-0.583), suggesting that states with shorter delivery times tend to generate
higher revenue. However, this relationship does not prove causation.

4. What percentage of customers make repeat purchases?
Among 93,358 customers with successfully delivered orders, only 2,801
customers made more than one purchase. This represents a repeat purchase rate
of approximately 3.00%.

The very low repeat purchase rate indicates that Olist relies heavily on
one-time customers and has a significant opportunity to improve customer
retention through loyalty programs, personalized promotions, and post-purchase
marketing.

5. What are the most commonly used payment methods?
Credit card is the dominant payment method, used in approximately 75.24% of
orders, followed by boleto at 19.46%. Voucher and debit card account for only
3.80% and 1.50%, respectively.

Credit card also has the highest average payment value at approximately
163.32 BRL. This indicates that maintaining a convenient and reliable credit
card payment experience is especially important for Olist.

6. How does delivery performance affect review scores?
Orders delivered on time or early receive an average review score of
approximately 4.29, while late orders receive only 2.57. This represents a
difference of approximately 1.73 points.

The correlation between delivery delay and review score is -0.267, indicating
that longer delays are associated with lower customer ratings. Although other
factors may also affect reviews, improving on-time delivery is likely to
increase customer satisfaction.

7. Which sellers have high sales but poor customer ratings?
Using the top 25% of seller revenue, a minimum of 20 reviewed orders, and an
average review score below 3.5 as selection criteria, 24 high-sales sellers
were identified as having poor customer ratings.

The highest-revenue seller in this group generated approximately 186,570 BRL
from 973 orders but received an average rating of only 3.50. Some sellers had
even lower ratings, including one seller with an average score of approximately
2.39 across 107 reviewed orders.

Olist should investigate these sellers' product quality, delivery performance,
order accuracy, and customer complaints before continuing to promote them.

8. What actions can Olist take to improve revenue and customer retention?
Based on the analysis, Olist should consider the following actions:
 Improve customer retention:
Only approximately 3.00% of customers make repeat purchases. Olist should
introduce loyalty rewards, personalized promotions, discount vouchers for
second purchases, and post-purchase email campaigns.

Improve delivery performance:
Late orders receive an average review score of only 2.57, compared with 4.29
for orders delivered on time or early. Olist should prioritize logistics
improvements in high-revenue states with longer delivery times, especially
RJ and BA.

Monitor seller quality:
Twenty-four high-sales sellers were identified with average ratings below
3.5. Olist should monitor their delivery performance, product quality,
order accuracy, and customer complaints.

Prioritize high-performing product categories:
Health and beauty, watches and gifts, and bed, bath and table generate the
highest revenue. Marketing campaigns and product recommendations should
prioritize these categories while protecting product availability.

Optimize payment experience:
Credit cards account for approximately 75.24% of payment usage. Olist should
maintain a reliable credit card checkout process, support secure installment
payments, and use voucher incentives to encourage repeat purchases.

Reduce geographic dependency:
São Paulo contributes approximately 37.41% of total revenue. Olist should
retain its strong position in SP while improving delivery and targeted
marketing in other high-potential states to diversify revenue.
